# Administrative boundaries

Simplified ADM1 boundaries from [geoBoundaries gbOpen](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api.html), release commit `9469f09`.

- China: geoBoundaries / Wikimedia Commons. Source metadata identifies the original boundary license as Public Domain.
- Japan: geoBoundaries / OpenStreetMap contributors. Original boundary license: [Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). This bundled derived boundary database is distributed under ODbL 1.0, separately from the MIT source code. Rings were simplified with 0.008° tolerance and coordinates rounded to five decimal places. geoBoundaries collection attribution: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- geoBoundaries: Runfola et al. (2020), “geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries”, PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231866

These generalized boundaries are for visualization and are not authoritative survey or navigation data. Business indicators in templates are demonstration data.

Satellite imagery is served on demand by Esri World Imagery with visible attribution. Street tiles are served by OpenStreetMap under its tile usage policy. Neither imagery nor street tiles are bundled or prefetched for offline use.
